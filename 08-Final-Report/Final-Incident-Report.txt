------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------

A suspicious email impersonating Microsoft Account Security
was investigated using the provided raw .eml email evidence.

The email claimed that unusual sign-in activity had been
detected on the recipient's account and instructed the
recipient to review recent activity.

The investigation included email header analysis,
SPF/DKIM/DMARC analysis, IOC extraction, URL investigation,
domain/DNS investigation, and IP threat-intelligence checks.

Based on the combined evidence, the email was classified as
PHISHING.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. KEY FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------

Finding 1:
The sender used a domain that impersonates Microsoft's
legitimate online account/security services.

Finding 2:
The email used an urgent account-security theme involving
unusual sign-in activity.

Finding 3:
SPF authentication failed.

Finding 4:
DKIM authentication failed.

Finding 5:
DMARC authentication failed.

Finding 6:
The email contained a shortened Bitly URL.

Finding 7:
VirusTotal reported 1/91 detections for the investigated
URL, with Phishing Database identifying the URL as phishing.

Finding 8:
The sending IP 178.238.225.91 was associated with
Contabo GmbH / AS51167. The IP itself was not currently
flagged as malicious by VirusTotal.

Finding 9:
The IP address 91.234.99.42 appeared inside the email body
as the alleged unusual sign-in IP. This was treated as an
investigation artifact and not automatically confirmed as
attacker infrastructure.

9. SOCIAL ENGINEERING INDICATORS
------------------------------------------------------------

The email used several phishing characteristics:

- Microsoft impersonation
- Urgent subject line
- Account-security theme
- Unusual sign-in warning
- Request to review account activity
- Suspicious sender domain
- Shortened URL
- Failed email authentication


------------------------------------------------------------
10. FINAL ASSESSMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

VERDICT:
PHISHING

CONFIDENCE:
HIGH

REASON:

The email combines multiple phishing indicators including
Microsoft impersonation, an urgent account-security theme,
SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication failures, a suspicious sender
domain, and a shortened URL that received a phishing
detection during threat-intelligence analysis.


------------------------------------------------------------
11. RECOMMENDED SOC RESPONSE
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Do not click the suspicious URL.

2. Quarantine or remove the phishing email.

3. Block the identified malicious URL/domain where
   appropriate.

4. Search the environment for other emails containing the
   identified IOCs.

5. Check whether the recipient clicked the URL.

6. If the user interacted with the phishing page, investigate
   possible credential exposure.

7. Review relevant identity-provider and endpoint logs for
   suspicious activity.

8. Reset credentials if compromise is confirmed or suspected.

9. Enable or verify MFA where appropriate.

10. Document the incident and preserve the original email
    evidence.


------------------------------------------------------------
12. CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------

The investigated email was classified as phishing based on
the combined evidence collected during the investigation.
